# Changelog

## v0.0.4 beta-2 — Bug fixed

Android clean-up after beta-1: stray "&" in track subtitles, feeds switching to
English, dates and numbering fixed; calmer Hairline without glow; seasonal effects,
three new visualizers, lyrics without the player, a welcome screen after signing in,
and redone Downloads, My files and History. Details in the
[release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.4 beta-1 — w0y with desktop

The computer version grows up: Windows is new, Linux is rebuilt in the Hairline
look with a side bar, a wide player bar, eight settings tabs, a mini player and
the built-in libmpv sound. Encrypted session, one-file backup, updates from
GitHub, local diagnostics. Android only gets small fixes. Details in the
[release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.3 beta-3

A new player (Framed cover and Poster layouts, the cover flies into the mini
player), the contour-map background, updates straight from GitHub with a
"What's new" window, Quick picks on Home, a yearly recap, and the polish pass:
visible search errors with Copy debug info, a connection test, delete
protection, recommendations that do not repeat, tabbed settings, a Hairline
widget. Details in the [release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.3 beta-2

Hairline grows up: a new floating dock, tabbed settings, a bento "Yours" screen,
fully customisable playlists and folders, app background modes, seasonal
events, and a w0y icon in the notification shade. Details in the
[release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.3 beta-1

New Hairline look, your own audio files, lyrics from several sources with an
adjustable delay, video for YouTube tracks, and a Linux version that catches
up with the phone. Details in the [release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.2 beta-2

Fixes for beta-1 and the Easy Glass look; split bars and grouped settings.

## v0.0.1 beta-1

First public beta.

- Android app (8.0+): search, playback with queue and sleep timer, account
  sign-in with two-way sync of playlists and likes, downloads (with saving to
  `Music/w0y music`), synchronised lyrics with translation, your own
  speed/pitch/reverb per track, clean mode, speed dial, stats, widget, themes
  and colour schemes, four languages.
- Linux desktop version (Compose Desktop, sound through `mpv`).
