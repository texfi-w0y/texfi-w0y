# Changelog

## Unreleased (draft for beta-3, not tagged)

Search errors are now visible with a reason, plus Copy debug info and a
connection test (no data leaves the phone).

Polish pass: the bottom island now sits in the layout (the screen ends above it,
not under it); settings are back to eight tabs with search behind a magnifier; delete
protection (long-press, confirm, UNDO) and handle-only reordering; your own files stay
in playlists after a sync; stats update by themselves, count a play after 30 seconds
and are restored from your history; three separate player panels (sleep timer, queue
with similar, more) that close without collapsing the player; recommendations that do
not repeat and show one shared reason; a Hairline widget in three sizes; crashes are
recorded locally and appear in Copy debug info.

## v0.0.3 beta-2

Hairline grows up: a new floating dock, tabbed settings, a bento "Yours" screen,
fully customisable playlists and folders, app background modes, seasonal
events, and a w0y icon in the notification shade. Details in the
[release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.3 beta-1

New Hairline look, your own audio files, lyrics from several sources with an
adjustable delay, video for YouTube tracks, and a Linux version that catches
up with the phone. Details in the [release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## v0.0.2 beta-2

Fixes for beta-1 and the Easy Glass look; split bars and grouped settings.

## v0.0.1 beta-1

First public beta.

- Android app (8.0+): search, playback with queue and sleep timer, account
  sign-in with two-way sync of playlists and likes, downloads (with saving to
  `Music/w0y music`), synchronised lyrics with translation, your own
  speed/pitch/reverb per track, clean mode, speed dial, stats, widget, themes
  and colour schemes, four languages.
- Linux desktop version (Compose Desktop, sound through `mpv`).
